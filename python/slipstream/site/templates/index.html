Buying or selling crypto in one big order pushes the price against you, and every exchange also takes a fee.

Every hour, Slipstream paper-trades a small order on the live Kraken and Coinbase order books, with four splitting strategies, and records what each one really cost, fees included.

No real money moves; the prices are real, only the fills are simulated.
